# Meridian Timeline Marketing Brief

Release: 3.4.15

## Positioning

Meridian helps Obsidian users explore the dates already in their notes as a connected, navigable chronology.

## Short description

Turn dated Obsidian notes into an interactive timeline. Filter and explore events, then open each event's source note.

## Product description

Meridian reads configurable date properties and supported date text, then presents point events and spans on a timeline. Search, filters, grouping, zoom, and saved views help users explore projects, research, and personal history without changing their notes.

Dates that are approximate, uncertain, conflicting, missing, or malformed stay visible as such. The plugin does not use AI to guess dates, and it does not send note contents to Constance. Three successful timeline operations are free per local calendar day; purchased use and checkout require separate billing metadata over the network.

## Best suited for

- Obsidian users whose notes already contain dates or date ranges.
- Researchers and students organizing historical events and source notes.
- Project teams reviewing milestones stored as Markdown notes.
- Journal keepers exploring dated notes while keeping the vault as the source of truth.

## Verified product claims

- Reads dates from configurable properties and supported text formats.
- Opens the source note from a timeline event.
- Supports filtering, zooming, refresh, progress, and cancellation.
- Keeps source notes read-only and parses note content locally.
- Surfaces unclear dates for review instead of claiming false precision.

## Claim boundaries

Do not call Meridian a full calendar, task manager, AI date extractor, or cloud timeline. Avoid promising exact parsing for every date style or a fixed performance level. Distinguish local note processing from network requests used for entitlement, purchased-use, and checkout metadata.
